About Overhead

See the skies above you.

Overhead shows aircraft flying within a 10 km radius of your current location, giving you a simple way to discover the planes passing above.

The radar map displays live aircraft positions, while the flight details panel provides useful information such as callsign, route, aircraft type, registration, altitude, speed, heading, and distance from you.

How it works

  • LocationYour browser location centers the radar around you.
  • 10 km radiusOnly aircraft inside the detection area are shown.
  • Live updatesAircraft data refreshes automatically every 15 seconds.
